Academic publications

My academic research studies parties, legislatures and elections in Paraguay and the United States. The full list of citations is on Google Scholar.

Book

Carrizosa, Andrés. 2023. Skewing Chaos: The Role of Political Parties in Paraguay’s Legislature. Springer Series in Electoral Politics. Cham: Springer.

It studies how parties influence lawmaking in the Paraguayan Congress, a case of legislative chaos, and proposes adding that chaos to theories of legislatures so they explain more cases.

DOI 10.1007/978-3-031-18625-7

Journal article

Carrizosa, Andrés. 2018. “Paraguay 2017: Competencia política en el Legislativo, en las calles y en las urnas” [Paraguay 2017: Political competition in the Legislature, in the streets and at the polls]. Revista de Ciencia Política 38 (2): 335–360.

A review of Paraguay’s political year in 2017. The article is in Spanish.

DOI 10.4067/s0718-090x2018000200335

Working papers

Duarte, Raúl, and Andrés Carrizosa. 2023. “Getting a seat at the (electoral) table: Partisan poll workers and electoral bias”. APSA Preprints.

Do party poll workers tilt the results? Using data from Paraguay’s 2018 general election, we find that they reduce their rivals’ votes and slightly increase their own party’s.

DOI 10.33774/apsa-2022-fd0gv-v5

Carrizosa, Andrés. 2020. “The Dimensionality of Cosponsorship Behavior in the House of Representatives”. APSA Preprints.

Using Bayesian item response models, it shows that bill cosponsorship in the U.S. House of Representatives has more than one dimension, unlike roll-call votes.

DOI 10.33774/apsa-2020-89mm1-v2
