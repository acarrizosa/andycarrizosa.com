Andy Carrizosa

Data, politics, finance, and economics. Based in Asunción, Paraguay.

I use data to tell stories. I am interested in politics, economics, and the financial system. Focused on analyzing Paraguay, the United States, and Latin America. I run LegisLab.org, a site focused on bringing together legislative data for the country and the region.

I have a Ph.D. in Political Science from Rice University. I have private-sector experience in market research, financial research, user experience research, and Big Data analysis using Python, R, and SQL.
